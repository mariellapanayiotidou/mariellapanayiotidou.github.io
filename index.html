The art of Dance

Dance [n.]: the art of turning feelings into movements when words are too small to hold them. A language spoken by the soul, written in the air, and remembered by the body long after the music fades. A thousand emotions woven into a single movement, a thousand stories told without a single word. Somewhere between the first note and the final bow, the world falls silent, and for a moment, everything that cannot be said becomes something that can be felt.
